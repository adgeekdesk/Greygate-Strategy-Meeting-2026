Procurement,
Inventory & QC

Procurement leads: agree approval turnaround targets under the new Delegation of Authority Matrix, minimum stock/reorder rules, and how QC1–QC6 ownership is enforced without slowing work down.

45 min · Ends with a 10-minute decisions capture

[ Scroll ]
[ Current gap ]

Procurement timing and approvals, and inventory control, are named directly as sore points. A root cause is structural, not just procedural: with no Finance Department, procurement approvals and supplier payments run through the CEO alone, so Procurement is regularly slow or strained waiting on a single person's availability. The QC framework (QC1–QC6, Appendix A of the Master SOP) is thorough on paper but depends on procurement and workshop discipline to function.

[ Discussion points ]
  1. 01Agree standard approval turnaround times for purchase requests by value band (e.g. under a defined threshold vs. above it), and who may approve at each band — this should draw directly on the Delegation of Authority / Approval Matrix agreed in Pillar 1, so Procurement is not waiting solely on the CEO.
  2. 02Agree minimum stock levels and reorder triggers for recurring/consumable materials to reduce reactive procurement.
  3. 03Confirm the QC Materials Decision Guide (already diagrammed in the Master SOP) is being used at the point of receiving, and agree a corrective path when materials fail it.
  4. 04Agree how QC sign-off authority is enforced at each of the six QC layers so nothing proceeds to the next stage without sign-off — including subcontractor-executed work.
  5. 05Agree how procurement, workshop and installation feed a single materials list per Job Card, so variations and substitutions are tracked, costed and approved rather than informal.
  6. 06Agree that once Finance confirms a supplier payment, Procurement is notified directly rather than finding out informally or after the fact.

Appendix B · Objective 6 — Departmental Efficiency (Procurement, QC & Finance)

Materials, quality and money never bottleneck a project — and everyone who needs to know, knows.

KR 1Payment-to-project-start visibility workflow live: Finance confirms payment, Procurement and Projects are notified automatically
KR 2Procurement approval turnaround meets agreed targets on ≥90% of requests
KR 3Zero fabricated or installed work proceeds without QC sign-off (QC1–QC6) on file

[ Decision needed ]

Approve turnaround-time targets and reorder rules, tied to the Delegation of Authority Matrix; assign an owner to implement the QC sign-off enforcement across all six layers.
