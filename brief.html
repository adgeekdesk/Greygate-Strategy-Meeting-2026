AttendeeStanding role (per Master SOP)Role in this strategy meeting

Note: the current Master SOP does not yet name a Finance role or a formal Human Resources function, and no Finance Department exists in practice today. Formalizing Finance — including payment processing and visibility — is the recommended priority agenda outcome.
