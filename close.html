[ What leadership leaves with ]Meeting objectives

By the close of this session, the leadership team should have agreed on:

  • A revised organizational structure with clear reporting lines, role accountability and decision-making authority (including CEO and Creative Director override matrices already referenced in the Master SOP).
  • Agreement to formally establish a Finance Department — with payment processing and payment-status visibility as core, immediate responsibilities — and a written Delegation of Authority / Approval Matrix that removes the CEO as the sole approver across Procurement, Planning, Execution, Logistics and Inventory.
  • A prioritized list of policies and SOPs to formalize, pilot and roll out — including ownership, timelines and a review cadence.
  • Agreed fixes to the process flow bottlenecks between Sales, Design, Procurement, Workshop, Installation and Subcontractors, particularly around task assignment, approvals and inventory control.
  • An agreed approach to project planning and management — how jobs are scheduled, tracked and reported on from Job Card to handover — and a decision on evaluating and piloting a digital platform (such as Odoo) to run it.
  • A budgeting, forecasting and financial reporting framework, including a plan to bring P&L reporting and statutory/financial obligation forecasting current.
  • A resourcing and readiness plan for the new workshop and studio, sequenced against the operational fixes above rather than run in parallel and unmanaged.
  • A draft company-level OKR set for the coming quarter — one Objective and 2–4 Key Results per pillar — captured in Appendix B, with owners and deadlines logged in the Decision & Action Log (Appendix C).
  • A confirmed 10th-anniversary mantra to carry through internal culture and external communications.

[ 10th-anniversary alignment ]

The anniversary is the stated reason for timing this strategy process now. It is an opportunity to communicate the new operating discipline — internally to staff and externally to clients and subcontractors — as part of the milestone, rather than treating it as a separate marketing exercise.

  1. 01Confirm whether the anniversary communications (internal and client-facing) should reference the strengthened structure, SOPs and new facility as part of the milestone narrative.
  2. 02Confirm timing: should policy/SOP rollout and the anniversary be sequenced together for internal messaging impact.
