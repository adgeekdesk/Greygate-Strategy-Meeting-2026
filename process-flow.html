The stage flow is logical, but the business has named task assignment, project planning, resource allocation and time management as sore points — meaning the flow is not being followed consistently or is not supported by a live system.

[ Discussion points ]
  1. 01Confirm whether The Projects Management System referenced throughout the SOP is implemented, partially implemented, or still aspirational — this materially changes what “fixing” the process flow means in practice, and links directly to the Pillar 2 decision on Odoo.
  2. 02Agree a single point of task assignment: every task has one named owner, one due date, and one visible status. Until the digital platform (Pillar 2) is live, a manual daily-dashboard fallback should be agreed for the interim; once live, the platform becomes the single system of record.
  3. 03Agree how Gantt/capacity checks are actually enforced before Sales commits a timeline (Stage 2.1 in the SOP already requires this — confirm it is happening).
  4. 04Agree how the 3-changes-then-rebill rule (Stage 4.4) and the Variations process (Stage 3.1) are tracked so scope creep does not silently erode margin.
  5. 05Agree escalation triggers for delays — who is notified, at what threshold, and what happens next.
